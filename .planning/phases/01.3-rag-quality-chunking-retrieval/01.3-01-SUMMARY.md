## Decisions Made

1. **Docling classic pipeline over VLM:** Classic pipeline sufficient for regulatory documents with clear structure. VLM adds latency and complexity without measurable benefit for CCoP documents.

2. **Clause-level granularity:** Each chunk maps to one numbered clause (e.g., 5.2.1). This is non-negotiable for compliance Q&A where auditors need answers traced to specific clauses, not "somewhere in Section 5."

3. **Merge tiny chunks instead of dropping:** Clauses under 30 tokens (typically cross-references or one-liners) are merged with parent clause rather than dropped. Prevents losing important cross-reference context.

4. **Deterministic chunk IDs:** Format {source}::{clause_number} (e.g., "CCoP 2.0::5.2.1") generates consistent uuid5-based Qdrant point IDs. Enables re-indexing without duplicates, human-readable for debugging.

5. **Parent hierarchy in metadata only:** Parent path (e.g., "Chapter 5 > Section 5.2 > 5.2.1") stored in ChunkMetadata.parent_path but NOT prepended to chunk text. Per CONTEXT.md: hierarchy prepending is deferred to Experiment 4 (contextual chunking) after baseline is measured.

6. **Legacy code preservation:** PyMuPDF parser (ccop_pdf_parser.py) and section chunker (section_chunker.py) remain unchanged in codebase. New implementations coexist for rollback safety.
