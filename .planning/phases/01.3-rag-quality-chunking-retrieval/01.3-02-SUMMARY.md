## Decisions Made

- **Remove rag_similarity_threshold entirely:** The 0.6 threshold was designed for cosine similarity (0-1 range) but applied to RRF scores (0.001-0.033 range), causing 100% document filtering. Cross-encoder scores are logits (unbounded), making thresholds inappropriate. Grading is now measurement-only.
- **Cross-encoder model selection:** ms-marco-MiniLM-L12-v2 balances accuracy and speed. MS MARCO benchmarks show 25-35% precision@3 improvement over bi-encoder-only retrieval.
- **Funnel shape (20→3):** Retrieve 20 documents with fast bi-encoder, rerank with slow cross-encoder, keep top 3 for LLM. Separates recall (bi-encoder) from precision (cross-encoder).
- **Lazy model loading:** Thread-safe singleton pattern defers 400MB model load until first use, avoids loading during imports/testing.
- **Deprecate rag_grading_enabled:** LLM-as-judge grading removed (slow, inconsistent with local models). Grading is now always measurement-only.
