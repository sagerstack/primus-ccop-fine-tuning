## Decisions Made

1. **0-3 anchored scale for all dimensions**: Matches criteria-establishment.md Component 2 exactly. Enables discrete level descriptions with concrete CCoP-specific examples at each anchor. Not continuous 0-1 (no anchors) or 1-5 (wrong scale).

2. **Binary 0/3 for safety dimensions (B20, B21)**: Over-specification and hallucination are pass/fail — any fabrication = failure. No partial credit maintains safety threshold.

3. **Chain-of-Thought in all prompts**: "Think step-by-step about the response quality" instruction increases transparency and justification coherence per LalaEval methodology.

4. **Skip-and-flag error pattern**: judge_error=True with overall_score=0.0 and empty dimensions. No fallback conservative scores (the current try/except returning 3/3/3 is wrong — hides failures). Errors flagged for manual review.

5. **DimensionScore as separate dataclass**: Clean separation between single dimension (name, score, weight) and aggregate evaluation. Self-documenting, type-safe, extensible.

6. **Rubric templates in docs/ not code**: evaluation-rubrics.md is single source of truth. Code references it, doesn't embed prompts. Enables non-engineer rubric iteration.

7. **Weighted average normalization formula**: `sum(d.score × d.weight) / (3.0 × sum(d.weight))` normalizes 0-3 to 0-1 for aggregation. Supports multi-dimension benchmarks with different weights.
