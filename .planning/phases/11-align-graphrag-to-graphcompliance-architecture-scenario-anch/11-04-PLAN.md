<objective>
Policy Graph Stage 1 (text classification) and Stage 2 (rule formalization) per GraphCompliance §3.1 — this is where Compliance Units are MINTED. Classify each source semantic unit three ways (premise / meta-CU / actor-CU), mint the typed `:ComplianceUnit` node linked to its source clause, and formalize each obligation CU into the 4-tuple ⟨subject, constraint, context, conditions⟩.

Per D-07 (corrected), a CU is a GraphCompliance OBLIGATION, not a clause — CU identity and count are the OUTPUT of this pass, EMERGENT, never reconciled against the operative-leaf arithmetic. A premise clause yields no obligation CU; a clause may spawn more than one CU.
Output: minted `:ComplianceUnit` nodes typed premise/meta-CU/actor-CU, each obligation CU carrying a validated 4-tuple, each linked to its source clause's verbatim text + namespaced citation.
</objective>
