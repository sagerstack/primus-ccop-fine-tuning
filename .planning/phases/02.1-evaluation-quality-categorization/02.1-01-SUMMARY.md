## Decisions Made

**1. Quality group structure:**
- Three groups: Retrieval Quality (context_recall, context_precision), Model-RAG Grounding (faithfulness), Model Response Quality (llm_judge, answer_correctness, answer_relevancy)
- LLM Judge sits inside Model Response Quality, not as separate group (matches rubric evaluation nature)

**2. Aggregation approach:**
- Per-benchmark: simple average across test cases within each benchmark
- Overall: category-weighted average using existing EvaluationCategory weights (prevents over-weighting categories with more benchmarks like Compliance B6-B12)
- Two-step overall aggregation: Step 1 computes category-level group averages, Step 2 computes weighted sum normalized by total weight

**3. Data structure:**
- quality_categories as Dict[str, Any] (not nested Pydantic models) to avoid serialization complexity
- Structure: {"overall": {"groups": [...]}, "by_benchmark": {"B1": {"groups": [...]}, ...}}
- Each group has: {"name": str, "metrics": [{"name": display_name, "value": float|None}], "average": float|None}

**4. Error handling:**
- RAGAs evaluation_error=True: count as 0.0 in averages (simple math, no None propagation)
- llm-only mode: Retrieval Quality and Model-RAG Grounding groups show N/A (check rag_only_groups list)
